# Writing Portfolio — Brittany Nation

Original writing samples in my three lanes: **technical writing**, **content writing**, and **copywriting**. Each sample is a single clean page, written and built by hand in HTML/CSS with no frameworks.

**Live site:** https://brittanynation.github.io/writing-portfolio/

## The samples

| Sample | Lane | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [How to Reconcile a Bank Account in QuickBooks Online](https://brittanynation.github.io/writing-portfolio/technical-writing.html) | Technical writing | A step by step user guide with a before you begin checklist, the full reconcile procedure, and a troubleshooting section for a difference that will not reach zero, a missing transaction, and a wrong beginning balance. |
| [5 Bookkeeping Mistakes That Cost Small Businesses Money](https://brittanynation.github.io/writing-portfolio/content-writing.html) | Content writing | An SEO style article for small business owners: five common bookkeeping mistakes, why each one costs money, and the practical fix for each. |
| [Honeybee Bakery: Landing Page and Welcome Emails](https://brittanynation.github.io/writing-portfolio/copywriting.html) | Copywriting | Landing page copy (headline, benefits, testimonial block, call to action) plus a three email welcome sequence (welcome, story, offer) for a fictional neighborhood bakery. |

## Roles this supports

Built to support applications for **Technical Writer**, **Content Writer**, and **Copywriter** roles: documentation and how-to writing, educational articles for a business audience, and warm, conversion-minded marketing copy.

## Files

- `index.html` — portfolio home, linking all three samples
- `technical-writing.html` — the QuickBooks Online reconciliation guide
- `content-writing.html` — the bookkeeping mistakes article
- `copywriting.html` — the Honeybee Bakery landing page copy and welcome emails
- `styles.css` — shared cream and ink stylesheet
