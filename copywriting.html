Copywriting Sample

Honeybee Bakery

Landing page copy and a three email welcome sequence for Honeybee Bakery, a fictional neighborhood bakery. The testimonial and offer details are written as part of the sample.

Part 1: Landing page copy

Honeybee Bakery

Baked at sunrise. Gone by noon.

Small batch breads, pastries, and cakes made from scratch every morning in your neighborhood. When it sells out, it is gone, and that is exactly how fresh should work.

See this week’s bakes

Out of the oven, not the freezer

Our bakers start at 4 in the morning so the first trays come out as the doors open. Nothing here was baked last week, thawed, or shipped in.

Ingredients you can pronounce

Flour, butter, eggs, local honey, fruit from farms we can name. The ingredient list on every loaf is short enough to read out loud.

Order ahead, skip the line

Reserve your Saturday sourdough and morning pastries online by Thursday night. We will have your box packed and waiting at the counter.

“The honey wheat loaf ruined store bought bread for our whole family. We plan our Saturdays around the bake box now.”

Sample testimonial written for this portfolio piece. Name withheld in sample.

Join the Honeybee list for the weekly bake menu, first dibs on seasonal specials, and a welcome treat on your first order.

Part 2: Welcome email sequence

Three short emails sent over the first week after someone joins the list. Warm first, useful second, and the offer comes last, after the bakery has earned a little trust.

Email 1 of 3: Welcome  |  Sends immediately
Subject: Welcome to the Honeybee list (your treat is inside)
Preview: Plus 10 percent off your first order.

Hi there,

Welcome to the Honeybee list. You just joined the group of neighbors who hear about our bakes first, including the seasonal ones that never make it past 9 in the morning.

Here is what to expect: one email a week, every Tuesday, with the bake menu for the weekend. No noise, no daily blasts. Just bread.

As a thank you, take 10 percent off your first order with the code WELCOME10. Use it online or mention it at the counter.

See you at sunrise,

The Honeybee Bakery team

Email 2 of 3: Our story  |  Sends on day 3
Subject: It starts at 4 a.m. with a starter named Beatrice
Preview: A short story about how your bread gets made.

Hi there,

Every Honeybee morning starts in the dark. The ovens come on at 4, the coffee goes on at 4:05, and Beatrice gets fed at 4:10.

Beatrice is our sourdough starter, and she has been alive longer than the bakery has. Every loaf of our sourdough begins with her, a bag of stone milled flour, water, salt, and time. No shortcuts, no commercial yeast doing the heavy lifting. Just patience and a warm kitchen.

That is the whole Honeybee philosophy, really. Good ingredients, handled slowly, by people who live three streets away from you. We think you can taste the difference, and we would love for you to test that theory.

Warmly,

The Honeybee Bakery team

Email 3 of 3: The offer  |  Sends on day 6
Subject: Your Saturday Bake Box is waiting
Preview: Order by Thursday night. We bake, you sleep in.

Hi there,

Saturday mornings at Honeybee have a line by 7:30. The Bake Box is how you skip it.

One box holds a full sourdough loaf, four seasonal pastries, and whatever the bakers are proudest of that week. Order by Thursday night, and your box is packed, labeled, and waiting at the counter whenever you wander in. Still warm, if you time it right.

Boxes are limited to what the ovens can honestly hold, and they do sell out. If you have been meaning to try us, this weekend is a good one to start.

Reserve my Bake Box

See you Saturday,

The Honeybee Bakery team
